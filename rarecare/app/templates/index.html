What RareCare focuses on

Three cancers where silence costs the most

Breast

Breast lumps, nipple discharge or retraction, and skin changes. Most breast lumps are benign, but every new lump warrants clinical assessment.

Cervical

Post-coital or intermenstrual bleeding and abnormal vaginal discharge. Symptoms that are often under-reported and benefit from early examination.

Ovarian

Abdominal distension, early satiety and pelvic pain. Non-specific symptoms where persistence is the key clinical signal.

Why it's different

Clinically grounded, private and transparent

  • 01

    Understands plain language

    Descriptions in everyday words are mapped to the correct clinical concepts, so you don't need medical vocabulary.

  • 02

    Asks fewer, gentler questions

    Only questions that change the outcome are asked, least personal first. "Prefer not to say" is always an option.

  • 03

    Says "I'm not sure" honestly

    When it can't tell, it asks or escalates rather than guessing. It never gives a false all-clear.

  • 04

    Shows its reasoning

    Every result traces back from your words to the medical guideline it's based on.

Private by design

No account. No history. Nothing you type is stored or logged. The tab is named "Notes", and Esc Esc leaves instantly.

          Research

          An ongoing research prototype

          RareCare studies three questions existing symptom checkers and imaging AI don't answer.

          Contribution A

          The lay-language gap

          How much do biomedical language models (BioBERT) degrade when symptoms are described in non-clinical language, and how much does lay-to-clinical alignment recover? Measured on the StigmaSymp-W benchmark.

          Contribution B

          Guaranteed sensitivity

          Conformal prediction bounds the rate of missed urgent cases, even when the scan and the symptoms were never collected together.

          Contribution C

          Minimal disclosure

          Questions are chosen by information gain relative to their sensitivity, reaching a confident result with the fewest personal questions.

          Status

          The system architecture, guideline graph, questioning agent, uncertainty layer and this app are built. BioBERT, SapBERT and ConvNeXt models are being trained; statistical results will be published after training and external validation.

          Not a medical device. Not clinically validated.

          Privacy

          Your words stay yours

          • No accounts, no history

            There's nothing to sign up for, and nothing is kept after you close the page.

          • Nothing stored or logged

            Your text and images are processed in memory and discarded. Server logs record only that a request happened, never its content.

          • Personal details removed first

            Phone numbers, emails and names are stripped before any model sees your words.

          • Discreet on shared phones

            The tab is titled "Notes". Press Esc twice or tap Quick exit to leave instantly.

          • Follow-up sessions expire

            If you're answering follow-up questions, the session lasts 15 minutes and then is deleted automatically.
